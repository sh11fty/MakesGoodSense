Who has to use it

Making Tax Digital for Income Tax applies to self-employed and rental income over £50,000 from April 2026, over £30,000 from April 2027 and over £20,000 from April 2028.

What changes

  • You send HMRC short updates every quarter, four a year
  • Your records are kept digitally in compatible software
  • The year-end return is still filed after the tax year

What we do

We prepare and send the quarterly updates, finish your year-end accounts and file your return, so you do not have to learn the software or chase the dates.
