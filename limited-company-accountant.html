Deadlines to know

  • Accounts are due at Companies House 9 months after your company’s financial year end.
  • Corporation tax is normally due 9 months and 1 day after the end of the accounting period.
  • The Company Tax Return is due 12 months after the end of the accounting period.

Missing them brings automatic penalties, so we put the dates in front of you early.
