Questions people ask.

What do you need from me?

Your bank statements, your sales records (invoices, takings or a spreadsheet) and your receipts. If you’ve had accounts done before, last year’s figures help. Not sure what counts? Ask and we’ll tell you.

My records are a bit of a mess. Is that a problem?

Tell us up front and we’ll say what’s needed. If tidying up goes beyond your fixed fee, we agree the cost with you before we start.

What does the fixed fee cover?

Everything listed in your package. If you want something extra, such as bookkeeping, VAT returns or payroll, we quote it separately and you say yes or no first.

What is Making Tax Digital?

It means sending HMRC short updates every quarter instead of one return a year. It applies to self-employed and rental income over £50,000 from April 2026, over £30,000 from April 2027 and over £20,000 from April 2028. If it applies to you, choose the sole trader + MTD package. Not sure? Ask and we’ll check.

Can you take over my accounts from someone else?

Yes. We’ll tell you what we need to pick up where things were left.

Do you work with people in Sheffield?

Yes. We’re based in Rotherham and work with clients in Sheffield and across South Yorkshire. Most things are done by phone, WhatsApp or email, so where you live makes no difference to the price.

Do I have to come in to see you?

No. Most things can be done by phone, WhatsApp or email. If you’d rather meet in person, just ask.
