# Google Business Profile and off-site SEO checklist

Google Business Profile, Search Console and directories need your Google login, so I can't do them for you. Everything below is ready to paste.

## 1. Google Business Profile (business.google.com)
- **Name:** Good Sense Accountancy (exactly as on the website, no keywords added)
- **Category (primary):** Accountant. **Additional:** Tax preparation service, Bookkeeping service, Tax consultant
- **Address:** do NOT enter a street address. Choose "I deliver goods and services to my customers" and hide the address. Never use an address you don't operate from
- **Service areas:** Rotherham, Sheffield, Wath-upon-Dearne, Maltby, Dinnington, Swinton, Barnsley, Doncaster
- **Website:** https://makesgoodsense.co.uk/
- **Phone:** use the same number as the website (still a placeholder on the site, fix it first)
- **Hours:** set the real ones
- **Description (750 max):**
  Fixed-price accountants in Rotherham, working with clients across Sheffield and South Yorkshire. We do accounts, self assessment and tax returns for sole traders, CIS subcontractors and small limited companies. Sole trader accounts from £395 a year, limited companies from £415. You deal with the same person every time, in plain English. Book a free 15-minute chat and get your fixed price within one working day.
- **Services (add each with price):** Sole trader accounts and tax return £395/yr; Sole trader with Making Tax Digital £545/yr; Limited company accounts from £415/yr; CIS tax refund and returns (quoted); Bookkeeping and VAT returns £50-£150/qtr; Payroll £10/month
- **Photos:** upload og.png as a cover, a logo, plus photos of you or the office
- **Booking/appointment link:** https://makesgoodsense.co.uk/#quote
- **Verify** by postcard, video or phone, whichever Google offers

### Reviews
After each job, send clients your Google review link (Profile > "Ask for reviews"). Reply to every review. Never offer incentives.

## 2. Search Console (search.google.com/search-console)
1. Add property > Domain > makesgoodsense.co.uk, add the DNS TXT record Google gives you
2. Sitemaps > submit `https://makesgoodsense.co.uk/sitemap.xml`
3. URL Inspection > Request indexing for the home page and both city pages
Also do Bing Webmaster Tools (import from Search Console).

## 3. Citations (use identical name, address, phone everywhere)
Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Yell, Thomson Local, FreeIndex, Cylex, Hotfrog, Facebook page, LinkedIn page, Companies House (if registered address matches).
Professional listings once you hold the registration: your AML supervisory body (ICAEW, ACCA, AAT, etc.) directory.

## 4. Do before launch (blocks trust and local SEO)
- Add the phone number to index.html once you have it (Number People line)
- Before taking clients: AML supervision registration, ICO registration, professional indemnity insurance. Add their numbers to the footer once issued
- Add `sameAs` links (GBP, LinkedIn, Facebook) to the JSON-LD on index.html once they exist
