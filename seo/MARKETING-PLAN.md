# Marketing plan (evenings and weekends, no cold selling)

Goal: 5 to 10 clients in the first 3 months, then build up. Roughly 2 hours a week.

## Month 0: before anything goes public
- AML supervision, ICO registration, professional indemnity insurance
- Check your employment contract for outside-work and conflict clauses
- Buy the Number People line, add the number to the site
- Create a free Calendly (or similar) page with 15-minute evening/Saturday slots, then swap the "Book your free call" link on index.html to it

## Month 1: get found (about 3 hours total)
1. Google Business Profile as a service-area business, address hidden (see GOOGLE-BUSINESS-PROFILE.md)
2. Search Console: verify domain, submit sitemap, request indexing of home and city pages
3. Bing Places, Yell, Apple Business Connect; same name, area, phone and website everywhere
4. Ask any friend, relative or past colleague for an honest Google review once you have a first piece of work

## Months 1-3: bring clients to you (2 hours a week)
- **Facebook groups and Reddit (1 hour/week):** join local groups for hairdressers, barbers, taxi/private hire, driving instructors, dog walkers, CIS/tradespeople. Answer tax questions helpfully, never pitch. Put your website in your profile only.
- **Referral emails (30 min/week):** send 3 short emails a week to people who meet new sole traders: driving schools, salon owners, trade counters, mortgage brokers, business bank managers, insurance brokers. Offer a thank-you for introductions if the rules of your AML supervisor allow it.
- **One useful page a month (30 min):** e.g. "What a taxi driver can claim", "CIS refund explained". Ask me to write and publish them.

## Optional: paid ads
Google Ads search campaign for "sole trader accountant Rotherham", "CIS accountant Sheffield", etc. Set a low daily cap, run it evenings and weekends only, pause when full. Treat it as a test and stop if it does not bring enquiries after a month or two.

## Every client
- Give a fast, friendly first call
- Ask for a Google review after the first successful job
- Ask who else they know who is self-employed

## Track
Enquiries, calls booked, clients won, and where each came from. Do more of what works.
